# Creating an account

## Overview

Before you get all excited about creating an account on our myTT service, you need to read the following guidelines and terms below before you start!

* Be at least 13 years of age (per the terms and conditions agreement).
* Have a verified email.
* Secure password that you can remember.
* Have some respect to others.
